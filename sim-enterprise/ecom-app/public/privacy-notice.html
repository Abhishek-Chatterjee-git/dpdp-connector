Digital Personal Data Protection Notice & Privacy Policy

This statutory notice is issued by Artisan Crafts Private Limited in compliance with Section 5 and Section 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2025 (DPDP Act).

1. Purpose and Categories of Personal Data Processed

We collect and process digital personal data exclusively under specified, lawful purposes consented to by you or permissible under applicable Indian law:

A. Essential Processing
Full Name, Delivery Address, Phone Number, and Payment references for order delivery, billing, and tax compliance (GST).
Mandatory
B. Promotional Offers & SMS
Direct mobile SMS and email alerts regarding seasonal discounts and flash sale campaigns.
Consensual / Revocable
C. Storefront Personalization
Pseudonymized browsing telemetry to recommend relevant artisan collections.
Consensual / Revocable

2. Real-Time Hot-Path Consent Verification & Zero-PII Egress

Our enterprise applications use a federated Two-Plane architecture. All marketing and non-essential transactions are inspected by a local, in-VPC Zone Agent in real-time ($< 1\text{ms}$). Raw customer identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, Card credentials) remain strictly within local encrypted databases and are never transmitted to external SaaS platforms.

3. Your Statutory Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act 2025, you possess enforceable legal rights:

  • Right to Access (Section 11): Download a complete machine-readable copy of your personal data and processing history.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Request immediate erasure of your data across all transactional and analytical databases.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6(4)): Withdraw consent for marketing or analytics with instant effect without penalty.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
